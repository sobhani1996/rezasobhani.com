<idea>
**Design Movement**: **Swiss Style (International Typographic Style)**

**Core Principles**:
1.  **Objectivity & Clarity**: The content (research) is the hero; the design is the invisible vessel.
2.  **Grid Systems**: A strict, mathematical grid to organize complex information (publications, data).
3.  **Asymmetry**: Dynamic layouts that create visual interest without clutter.
4.  **Sans-Serif Typography**: Clean, legible type for high readability of academic text.

**Color Philosophy**:
*   **Clinical Blue & Slate**: Reflecting the medical and scientific nature of the work (Nutrition, Public Health).
*   **High Contrast Black/White**: For maximum readability of text.
*   **Accent Red**: Used sparingly for calls to action (e.g., "Download CV", "View Publication") to guide the eye.
*   *Reasoning*: Evokes trust, precision, and scientific rigor.

**Layout Paradigm**:
*   **Modular Grid**: Content is broken into distinct blocks or modules.
*   **Split Screen**: One side for navigation/context, the other for deep-dive content.
*   **Whitespace as Structure**: Large margins and padding to separate sections, not lines.

**Signature Elements**:
1.  **Bold Section Numbers**: Large, oversized numbers (01, 02, 03) to index sections like chapters in a thesis.
2.  **Geometric Shapes**: Subtle circles or squares representing data points or nutritional elements.
3.  **Monochrome Photography**: Profile and background images in black and white to maintain visual consistency.

**Interaction Philosophy**:
*   **Hover Reveals**: detailed information appears on hover to keep the initial view clean.
*   **Smooth Scrolling**: Linear progression through the "story" of the academic career.

**Animation**:
*   **Parallax Scrolling**: Subtle movement of background elements relative to foreground text.
*   **Fade-in Up**: Content blocks slide up and fade in as the user scrolls.

**Typography System**:
*   **Headings**: **Helvetica Now Display** (or Inter/Roboto as web safe) - Bold, tight tracking.
*   **Body**: **Inter** or **Roboto** - Clean, neutral, highly readable.
*   **Hierarchy**: Extreme contrast in size between headings and body text.
</idea>

**Design Movement**: **Organic Modernism / Biophilic Design**

**Core Principles**:
1.  **Nature-Inspired**: Reflecting the "Nutrition" and "Sustainable Diet" aspects of the research.
2.  **Softness & Fluidity**: Avoiding harsh lines in favor of curves and organic shapes.
3.  **Texture & Warmth**: Making academic content feel accessible and human-centric.
4.  **Sustainability**: Visuals that evoke environmental consciousness.

**Color Philosophy**:
*   **Sage Green & Earth Tones**: Directly connecting to "Sustainable Diet" and "Environmental Sustainability".
*   **Warm Beige/Cream Backgrounds**: Easier on the eyes than stark white, feeling more "paper-like".
*   **Terracotta Accent**: Adding warmth and vitality, representing food and health.
*   *Reasoning*: Aligns perfectly with the specific research niche of sustainable nutrition.

**Layout Paradigm**:
*   **Fluid/Masonry**: Content flows naturally like a magazine layout rather than a rigid table.
*   **Overlapping Elements**: Images and text boxes slightly overlap to create depth and connection.
*   **Curved Containers**: Rounded corners (large radii) or organic blob shapes for images.

**Signature Elements**:
1.  **Organic Blobs/Shapes**: Background SVG shapes that look like water or leaves.
2.  **Botanical Illustrations**: Subtle line drawings of plants or food items as background textures.
3.  **Card-based Content**: Research papers presented as "cards" that feel tactile.

**Interaction Philosophy**:
*   **Gentle Floats**: Elements float slightly when hovered.
*   **Ripple Effects**: Buttons have a soft ripple or organic expansion on click.

**Animation**:
*   **Slow Morphing**: Background shapes slowly change form.
*   **Staggered Reveal**: List items (publications) appear one after another with a soft spring effect.

**Typography System**:
*   **Headings**: **Merriweather** or **Playfair Display** - A serif font that feels academic yet warm and editorial.
*   **Body**: **Lato** or **Nunito** - A humanist sans-serif that is friendly and legible.

<idea>
**Design Movement**: **Neo-Brutalism (Academic Edition)**

**Core Principles**:
1.  **Raw & Unfiltered**: Presenting data and facts without unnecessary decoration.
2.  **High Contrast**: Bold borders, stark shadows, and distinct separation of elements.
3.  **Functional Aesthetics**: Every design element has a clear purpose; nothing is just for "pretty".
4.  **Database/Archive Feel**: Treating the website as a digital archive of work.

**Color Philosophy**:
*   **Paper White & Ink Black**: The classic academic paper aesthetic.
*   **Highlighter Colors**: Neon Yellow, Cyan, or Pink used to "highlight" key text or active states, mimicking a student highlighting a textbook.
*   **Gray Backgrounds**: For secondary information panels.
*   *Reasoning*: Stands out in the academic space, feels modern, confident, and data-driven.

**Layout Paradigm**:
*   **Bento Box / Grid**: Strict, visible borders separating every piece of content.
*   **Sidebar Navigation**: A persistent, tool-like sidebar for quick access to different research areas.
*   **Dense Information**: Not afraid of high information density, similar to a dashboard.

**Signature Elements**:
1.  **Thick Borders**: 2px or 3px solid black borders around everything.
2.  **Hard Shadows**: Solid black shadows (no blur) offset from the element.
3.  **Monospace Accents**: Using code-like fonts for dates, citations, and metadata.

**Interaction Philosophy**:
*   **Instant Feedback**: Hover states are immediate and high-contrast (e.g., invert colors).
*   **Clicky Feel**: Buttons depress visually (transform: translate) to feel mechanical.

**Animation**:
*   **None or Very Fast**: Transitions are instant or extremely quick (0.1s). No soft fades.
*   **Marquee**: Scrolling text for announcements or latest news.

**Typography System**:
*   **Headings**: **Space Grotesk** or **Archivo Black** - Quirky, bold sans-serifs.
*   **Body**: **IBM Plex Mono** or **JetBrains Mono** (for data) mixed with **Inter** (for long text).
</idea>
